Renergizr Industries Pvt. Ltd.

Clean energy technology · Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh

About

Deep-tech clean energy

Renergizr Industries is a deep-tech clean energy startup. It has developed an indigenous, patented process technology for round-the-clock clean electricity generation using geothermal energy.

The company is incubated at the Startup Incubation and Innovation Centre at the Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur and the I-Hub Foundation at the Indian Institute of Management Sambalpur.

  • Recognition
    4 national awards for clean energy innovation
Services

What Renergizr offers

Geothermal power systems

Binary-cycle systems using organic refrigerants to generate electricity from geothermal hot water.

Clean energy gensets

Modular 5 kilowatt to 1 megawatt systems that generate electricity from wastewater and green hydrogen, replacing diesel gensets.

Monitoring & maintenance

Remote monitoring with predictive maintenance software for deployed systems.

Projects

Projects

Completed

5 Kilowatt Geothermal Prototype

Delhi · Partner: Renergizr Industries

Prototype geothermal power unit in Delhi, inaugurated in November 2022 by officials of the Ministry of New & Renewable Energy and the Geological Survey of India.

Completed

20 Kilowatt Geothermal Pilot

Manuguru, Telangana · Partner: Renergizr Industries

Field demonstration with Singareni Collieries Company Limited at a site with an estimated 122 megawatts geothermal potential, achieving 16 gigajoules of energy savings.

In development

Clean Energy Genset System

Pan-India · Partner: Renergizr Industries

Modular 5 kilowatt to 1 megawatt gensets generating zero-emission electricity from wastewater and green hydrogen, with noise below 45 decibels.

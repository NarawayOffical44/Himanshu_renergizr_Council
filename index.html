About the Council

Connecting India with the global energy community

Bharat Geothermal & Energy Council is a non-political, independent and impartial organisation. We engage in strategic dialogue with the energy leaders of today and tomorrow, build capabilities, and help shape an energy future that is clean, affordable and reliable.

Through national member committees, partners, programmes and Future Energy Leaders, we work on whole-energy-system challenges—from India’s untapped geothermal resources to renewable, clean and green technologies.

Why Geothermal

Round-the-clock clean power from the Earth

Geothermal energy has the lowest operational cost and carbon footprint among renewable sources, and its output does not depend on the time of day or the weather. It provides baseload power for a stable grid—24 hours a day, 365 days a year.

  • Capacity factor above 98%, versus 25–35% for solar and wind
  • Around five times the power density of solar
  • Smallest land footprint among renewable technologies
  • Applications in power, green hydrogen, heating and mineral recovery

How It Works

How projects reach the right partners

For Project Owners

Submit a project

  1. Share your brief

    Send us the project scope, location, energy requirement and timelines.

  2. Council review

    We assess feasibility, resource availability and technical requirements.

  3. Partner allocation

    We assign the project to a vetted partner with matching expertise.

  4. Delivery & oversight

    The partner delivers while the Council tracks milestones and outcomes.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What kind of projects does the Council take on?

Projects across geothermal, renewable energy, clean and green technology, energy innovation and sustainable infrastructure—from feasibility studies and pilots to megawatt-scale installations.

Who can submit a project?

Government bodies, public sector companies, companies, institutions and community organisations with an energy project in India.

Who can become a partner?

Organisations with proven expertise in one or more of our focus areas. Apply through the Become a Partner page.

Is the Council a for-profit company?

No. The Council is a Section 8 not-for-profit company. It is non-political, independent and impartial.

How is the Council funded?

Through membership fees from its member committees, and through support from national and international, government and private organisations.

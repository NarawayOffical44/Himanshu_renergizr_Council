Profile

Who we are

Bharat Geothermal & Energy Council is a Section 8 not-for-profit company working across the energy sector. The Council takes on energy projects and allocates them to a network of qualified partners, while connecting India with the global energy community.

We are non-political, independent and impartial. The Council is funded through membership fees from its member committees and through support from national and international, government and private organisations.

“To promote the sustainable supply and use of energy for the greatest benefit of all people.”

Mission of the Council
Vision

Advancing India’s place in the global energy transition

An independent energy community, with member presence in over 100 countries, that connects leaders, industries, governments and innovators to meet whole-energy-system challenges.

Mission

Sustainable energy for all people

To champion India’s geothermal potential and enable innovation and partnerships for renewable, clean and green energy that is affordable and reliable.

Objectives

What the Council works towards

Promote sustainable energy

Advance the sustainable use and supply of energy for the benefit of all.

Strategic dialogue

Engage with the energy leaders of today and the future, build capabilities and help shape the energy future.

Use every resource

Harness resources above and below ground for generating, storing and transporting energy for industrial, commercial and residential use.

Practical solutions

Develop practical solutions that help energy leaders make informed decisions.

Access to clean energy

Enable access to clean, affordable and reliable energy.

Humanising energy

Involve more people and communities in the energy transition.

Reliable data

Provide reliable data, tools and impartial commentary for decision-makers.

Member committees

Build national member committees, partners, programmes and a Future Energy Leaders community.

Geothermal for India

Bring India’s geothermal resources into the mainstream of its clean energy mix.
